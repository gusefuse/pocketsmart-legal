Delete your PocketSmart account

You can request deletion of your PocketSmart account and associated data at any time.

How to request deletion

Send an email to pcktsmart@gmail.com with the subject PocketSmart Account Deletion Request.

Send the request from the email address associated with your PocketSmart account and include your PocketSmart account email address. Do not send your password or one-time codes.

What will be deleted

After verifying the request, we will delete the PocketSmart account and associated user-entered data, including profile information, budgets, expenses, savings goals, and progress data stored for that account.

Processing time and retention

We will process a valid request within a reasonable period. Limited information may remain temporarily in encrypted backups or where retention is required by law, after which it will be deleted or anonymized.
