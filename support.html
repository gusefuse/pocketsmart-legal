PocketSmart Support

For help with your account, budgets, transactions, savings goals, or message imports, email pcktsmart@gmail.com. Describe the issue and your device platform. Never send passwords, verification codes, or full banking details.

Signing in

Use the email and password associated with your PocketSmart account. To reset your password, enter your email on the Log In screen and choose Forgot password.

Optional message imports

On iOS, message imports require your consent and a Message automation you configure in Apple Shortcuts. PocketSmart cannot access your SMS inbox. Turn imports off in Profile and disable or delete the automation in Shortcuts to stop forwarding messages. You can always enter transactions manually.

Privacy and account deletion

You can delete your account from Profile > Delete account. For help or deletion without the app, see the account-deletion instructions. Read the privacy policy for information about data handling.
